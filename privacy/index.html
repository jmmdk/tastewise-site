Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how the Tastewise app and the website gettastewise.com handle personal data. We collect as little as we can, we never sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Useful Apps ApS, Staghøjvej 23A, 8210 Aarhus V, Denmark. CVR: DK43969188. Email: hi@gettastewise.com.

3. Service providers

We use carefully chosen providers (data processors) that only process data on our instructions and under a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase (database, login and hosting of your account, onboarding and tasting data), hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • PostHog, EU cloud (product analytics and crash/error reports)
  • Resend (sending login-code emails)

When you sign in with Apple or Google, or buy a subscription through the App Store or Google Play, Apple and Google process your data under their own privacy policies.

4. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some providers are based in the United States or may process data there. In that case the transfer is covered by the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, onboarding and tasting data: as long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, we delete the data within 30 days (backups are overwritten within a further 30 days).
  • Analytics data and crash reports: up to 24 months, after which they are deleted or anonymised.
  • Purchase records: as long as required by accounting law.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing that happened before). You can delete your account directly in the app under Profile → Delete account, or email us at hi@gettastewise.com. See how to delete your account. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk, or to the authority in your own country.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access is limited to people who need it. Login uses one-time codes or Apple/Google, so we never store a password for you.

8. Age

Tastewise is made for adults and is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Website

gettastewise.com is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or ads. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
