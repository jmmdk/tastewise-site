Delete your Tastewise account

Last updated: 12 November 2026

You can delete your Tastewise account and all the data in it at any time. This works the same whether you signed in with email, Apple or Google, or never signed in at all.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Tastewise and tap Profile in the tab bar.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with Delete.

Your account is deleted right away, and the app starts over from the beginning.

No access to the app?

Email hi@gettastewise.com from the email address you use in Tastewise and ask us to delete your account. If you signed in with Apple or Google, tell us which one. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login (email address, and the link to Sign in with Apple or Google).
  • Your onboarding answers, plan, food list and settings.
  • Your tastings, streaks, challenges and progress.
  • Everything Tastewise stores on your phone, including reminders and any Live Activity.

What is kept

  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as accounting law requires.
  • Analytics data and crash reports are not linked to your name or email. They are deleted or anonymised after at most 24 months.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a Tastewise Pro subscription. Cancel it in your App Store subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions, so you are not charged again. You can also tap Manage subscription on the delete screen in the app.
