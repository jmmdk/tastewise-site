1. What Tastewise is, and what it is not

Tastewise is a self-help and habit tool for adults who are picky eaters. It is not medical treatment, therapy or nutritional advice, and it does not diagnose or treat ARFID or any eating disorder. If you think you may have an eating disorder, if you are losing weight, or if eating affects your health or daily life, please talk to a doctor, psychologist or dietitian.

  • Never taste a food you are allergic or intolerant to, and follow any advice from your doctor about your diet.
  • You decide which foods you try and how far you go. You can stop at any time.
  • Stop and seek help if you feel unwell. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
